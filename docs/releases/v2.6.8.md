<h2 id="cn-v2.6.8">问题修复</h2>

- 执行机剩余空间充足时，受保护的工作区缓存超过预算也可以启动其他模块的新 Issue。
- 缓存超预算时提前登记符合条件的空闲工作区，交由后台回收。真实磁盘空间不足或容量无法确认时，继续延后启动。

<h2 id="en-v2.6.8">Bug Fixes</h2>

- New issues in other modules can start when the executor has sufficient disk space, even if protected workspace caches exceed the budget.
- Register eligible idle workspaces for earlier background cleanup when caches exceed the budget. Continue deferring starts when disk space is low or capacity cannot be confirmed.
